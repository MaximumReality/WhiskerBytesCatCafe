# 🐾 Whisker Bytes: Cat Café Fun ☕🍪

**Whisker Bytes** is a cozy, bite-sized browser game where you run a chaotic little cat café.  
Choose your cat, serve treats, manage messes, and survive a single shift of adorable mayhem.

Built for **mobile**, **short play sessions**, and **maximum chill**.

🌐 **Play here:**  
https://maximumreality.github.io/WhiskerBytesCatCafe/

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

You play as one of four café cats:

- **Mochi** – Fast cook times  
- **Ginger** – Extra tips  
- **Luna** – Higher movement speed  
- **Jinx** – Customers have more patience  

Your goal is to:
- Take orders ☕🍪🍼  
- Serve customers before they lose patience  
- Manage trash levels 🗑️  
- Survive until the café closes  

The day ends automatically — no grinding, no stress.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

### Mobile (Primary)
- On-screen D-Pad: Move  
- **📢 Meow button**: Interact with stations, customers, TV, radio, trash, and window  

### Desktop (Limited)
- Touch / pointer interaction only  
- *(Designed mobile-first)*

---

## 🧠 Game Systems

- Cooking stations for drinks, cookies, and milk  
- Customer patience (critics appear sometimes!)  
- Trash management — don’t let it overflow  
- Radio & TV visual effects  
- Floating tips and feedback  
- Local high score saved in your browser  

---

## 📱 Designed For

- Waiting rooms  
- Bus rides  
- Short breaks  
- One-handed mobile play  
- Free, no ads, no logins  

If you can finish a coffee, you can finish a shift ☕🐱

## 🧩 Tech Details

- Single-file HTML game

- Touch-first controls

- No tracking
